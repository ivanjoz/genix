# Cash Flow (Flujo de Caja)

<!-- DOC-ID: page-purpose -->
## Page purpose

Cash Flow (`Flujo de Caja`, `flujo de efectivo`, `proyección de caja`) answers "how much cash will
the company have each day, and when does it run out?". It starts from today's balance of every
cash and bank account (`saldo inicial`) and adds, day by day, what the company will collect and
pay, for the next 13 weeks.

The page only reads: it does not pay, collect or book anything. Each amount comes from the page
that owns it (sales, purchase orders, expenses, payroll, loans), and is corrected there. The only
records created here are planned entries (`entradas planificadas`). Actual past movements are in
Cash & Banks (`Cajas & Bancos`).

<!-- DOC-ID: concepts -->
## Business concepts (Conceptos del negocio)

- A **commitment** is one future cash movement with a date: a sale to collect, an order or expense
  to pay, a payroll payment, a loan installment, a tax. Each one belongs to a row (`concepto`):
  Receivables (Cobranzas), Purchase orders (Órdenes de compra), Expenses (Gastos), Assets (Activos),
  Recurring bills (Gastos programados), Payroll net pay (Planilla neto), CTS, Payroll contributions
  (Aportes de planilla), Loan installments (Cuotas de préstamos), IGV, Income tax prepayment (Pago a
  cuenta renta), Nuevo RUS, Forecast collections (Cobranzas pronosticadas), Forecast purchases and
  expenses (Compras y gastos pronosticados), Forecast taxes (Impuestos pronosticados) and Planned
  (Planificado).
- The **layer** (`capa`) says how certain a commitment is. **Known (Conocido)** comes from an
  existing document: an unpaid sale, a closed payroll, a loan installment. **Scheduled
  (Programado)** is generated by a rule: a recurring bill's next period, a payroll month not closed
  yet. **Forecast (Pronóstico)** is what the company's history predicts for documents not recorded
  yet: future sales and unscheduled purchases and expenses, and the taxes on them. **Planned
  (Planificado)** is the user's own entries.
- The **forecast** (`pronóstico`) learns each site's daily sales and its spending per expense
  category from up to a year of history, with the commercial calendar (holidays, paydays). Its
  documents turn into cash with the site's **collection and payment times** (`plazos de cobro y
  pago`), measured from the last 180 days of payments: the share of the money that arrives on the
  document's day, a day later, and so on. Only documents from tomorrow on are forecast; today's and
  past ones are Known.
- A **lane** is the site (`sede`) the cash belongs to. **All the company (Toda la empresa)** adds
  every site. **Company lane (no site) (Empresa (sin sede))** holds what belongs to no site:
  taxes, payroll, loan installments, and accounts or documents without a site. So a site lane can
  look healthy while the company lane goes negative, because the bank account that pays payroll and
  taxes may belong to a site.
- Dollar amounts are converted to soles at the latest sell exchange rate (`tipo de cambio
  venta`), held flat for the whole horizon.

## Read the daily forecast (Ver el pronóstico diario)

### User intention (Intención del usuario)

Know whether cash will be enough over the next weeks, and on which day it runs short.

### Where to find it (Dónde encontrarlo)

**Finance & Accounting (Finanzas y Contabilidad) → Treasury (Tesorería) → Cash Flow (Flujo de
Caja)** at `/finance/cash-flow`, tab **Daily 13 weeks (Diario 13 semanas)**, or **Monthly 12
months (Mensual 12 meses)** for a year by calendar month. The **Site (Sede)** selector picks the
lane the table shows; **Include forecast (Incluir pronóstico)**, ticked by default, adds the Forecast
layer, and unticking it shows the balance from recorded documents and rules alone. The refresh
button recomputes the commitments and the forecast.

### Required information and prerequisites (Requisitos previos)

Nothing is required to open the page, but the forecast is only as good as the records: cash
accounts with a site and recent counts, due dates on sales and purchase orders, payroll contracts,
the company's tax regime and the SUNAT tax calendar.

### Business rules and rationale (Reglas y razón de negocio)

- The table starts with **Opening cash (Saldo inicial)**: today's balance of the lane's accounts.
  Each day's **Closing balance (Saldo final)** is the previous day's plus that day's flows. Rows
  appear only for concepts that move money in the window, plus a **Total** per row.
- The line above the chart says **Cash runs short on (La caja queda en negativo el)** with the
  first negative day (or month) of the selected lane, or **Cash stays positive for 13 weeks (La caja
  se mantiene positiva 13 semanas)** / **for 12 months (12 meses)**. While the forecast is computed
  the toolbar shows **Forecasting... (Pronosticando...)**.
- In the monthly view each column adds up the month's days (the first one is the rest of the
  current month) and its closing balance is the one of the month's last day.
- The chart draws the closing balance of every lane, whatever lane the table shows.
- How commitments are dated:
  - A sale, order, expense or asset is dated on its due date. An overdue order, expense or asset is
    projected as paid **today** (and warned). An overdue sale is projected when the site's
    collections usually arrive at that age: the middle of the collections that come later than the
    sale's age, or today when none come that late. A receivable overdue more than 90 days is left
    out, as probably uncollectible. Unpaid orders, expenses and assets dated more than a year ago
    appear as one amount, **Payables older than a year (Cuentas por pagar de más de un año)**, owed
    today. A dollar document stays in dollars and is restated at the current sell rate.
  - A recurring bill projects each future period; one without an amount uses the mean of its last
    3 periods, or is not projected.
  - Payroll: net pay on the company's payday; CTS by the 15th of May and November; AFP on the 5th
    business day of the next month; other contributions on the SUNAT due date. A closed payroll is
    Known; an unclosed month is projected by the payroll engine from the current staff and
    contracts.
  - Loan installments: what each pending installment still owes, on its due date.
  - IGV and income tax: computed for the last closed periods from their sales and purchases
    (expenses, purchase orders and assets), minus what was already paid and any saldo a favor; due
    on the SUNAT calendar date for the company's RUC. The IGV of each amount follows from its tax
    treatment (18 %, 10 %, or none for exonerated, unaffected and purchases without a comprobante);
    dollar documents are restated at SUNAT's sell rate of their comprobante's date. A period whose
    due date passed unpaid is owed today. Sales are all taxed at 18 % for now.
  - Forecast taxes: the current month and the following ones add the forecast sales and purchases
    to what the current month already recorded. Sales IGV uses the share of IGV in the closed
    months' sales; the crédito fiscal uses the share in the last year's purchases and expenses; the
    saldo a favor carries from month to month. Renta is the prepayment rate on net sales; a Nuevo
    RUS company pays its monthly cuota instead.

### Result and side effects (Resultado y efectos)

None. The page computes the forecast each time it opens or is refreshed. The browser keeps what the
forecast learned, so a later visit only learns the new days.

### Limitations (Limitaciones)

- The forecast has no range (best and worst case) yet: each day shows one expected amount.
- With less than a year of sales the forecast has no yearly season, and a site selling for less
  than 90 days has no weekly shape of its own; the warnings say so.
- Asset purchases (`activos`) are forecast from history together with the other spending; a
  specific future purchase is better planned as a planned entry.
- Detracciones, percepciones, retenciones and the annual renta settlement are not projected.
- The exchange rate does not change over the horizon.

### Common questions and vocabulary (Preguntas y vocabulario)

- ¿Cuándo me quedo sin caja? ¿Me alcanza el dinero para pagar la planilla?
- ¿Cuánto voy a cobrar y pagar las próximas semanas? ¿Por qué la sede está en positivo y la
  empresa en negativo?
- ¿Cuánta caja tendré en diciembre? ¿Cuánto IGV voy a pagar el próximo mes? ¿Cómo veo el flujo sin
  el pronóstico?
- Flujo de caja proyectado, cash flow, presupuesto de caja, liquidez, tesorería, flujo mensual,
  proyección anual.

## See the records behind a day (Ver el detalle de un día)

### User intention (Intención del usuario)

Understand which documents make up an amount in the table.

### Where to find it (Dónde encontrarlo)

Click a day (or month) cell of any concept row. A side panel titled with the concept and the date
lists each **Record (Registro)**, its **Date (Fecha)**, its **Due (Vence)** date, its **Layer (Capa)**
and its **Amount S/ (Monto S/)**, outflows in red. For sales, purchase orders, expenses and assets
the panel first shows **Loading the documents... (Cargando los documentos...)** and then lists each
document with what it still owes, such as `Venta 1520` or `OC 88`; its date is the document's date.

### Business rules and rationale (Reglas y razón de negocio)

- The due date can be earlier than the cell's date: an overdue document shows its original due date
  while it is projected later.
- With a site selected, only that site's documents are listed; with **All the company (Toda la
  empresa)**, every site's.
- A forecast row shows one entry per site and day, such as **Collection of forecast sales (Cobro de
  ventas pronosticadas)**; a forecast tax shows its month, such as `IGV 2612`.

### Limitations (Limitaciones)

The panel only lists the records; correct them on their own page.

### Common questions and vocabulary (Preguntas y vocabulario)

- ¿De dónde sale este monto? ¿Qué facturas vencen ese día? ¿Qué compone el pago de ese día?

## Review the forecast warnings (Revisar las alertas del pronóstico)

### User intention (Intención del usuario)

Find the missing or inconsistent data that makes the forecast less reliable, and fix it.

### Where to find it (Dónde encontrarlo)

The **warnings (alertas)** button next to the site selector (red when there are high-severity
warnings) opens **Forecast warnings (Alertas del pronóstico)**. Each warning shows what the
forecast assumed, the amount at stake, the first affected records, and **Fix (Corregir)**, which
opens the page where it is corrected.

### Business rules and rationale (Reglas y razón de negocio)

Warnings are grouped as **High (Alta)**, **Medium (Media)** and **Info (Informativa)**, and ordered
by amount at stake. A warning about sales, orders, expenses or assets lists the affected days and
sites (for example `05-11-2026 · Central`), not each document: open that day in the table to see
them. Each warning states the assumption the forecast made instead, for example:

- No SUNAT calendar for a period: its taxes and contributions fall due on the 15th of the next
  month.
- No tax regime on the company: no income tax prepayment is projected.
- Overdue orders, expenses, assets or loan installments: projected as paid today. Unpaid orders,
  expenses or assets older than a year: projected as paid today too.
- Receivables overdue more than 90 days: left out.
- Unpaid sales without a due date, or purchase orders without a payment date: due on their own date.
- Sales or expenses of the last months without a site: counted in the company lane.
- Clients owing money on cash terms (`contado`): their sales fall due on the sale date.
- Purchases without a comprobante: no IGV credit, so the IGV payable is overstated.
- No payday in the company calendar: payroll is paid on the last day of the month.
- Active employees without a contract in force: left out of the payroll projection.
- Last month's payroll not closed: projected with the payroll engine.
- Collections (`Cobros`) booked against loans received (451): a loan without a schedule, whose
  repayments are unknown.
- Accounts with a negative balance, without a count in the last 30 days, or without a site; dollar
  amounts with no exchange rate in the last 7 days (dollars taken 1 to 1); planned entries dated in
  the past.
- About the forecast, with nothing to fix on another page (no Fix button): less than a year of
  sales (no yearly season); sites selling for less than 90 days (no weekly shape of their own);
  sites with few document payments in 180 days, whose forecast cash uses the company's collection
  or payment times, or the document's day when there are none.

### Result and side effects (Resultado y efectos)

None until the user corrects the data on the linked page; the forecast changes on the next refresh.

### Common questions and vocabulary (Preguntas y vocabulario)

- ¿Por qué el pronóstico tiene alertas? ¿Qué significa "sin calendario SUNAT"? ¿Cómo corrijo una
  alerta?

## Plan one-off movements (Entradas planificadas)

### User intention (Intención del usuario)

Add a future movement no document or rule predicts: buying equipment, paying dividends, an owner's
capital contribution.

### Where to find it (Dónde encontrarlo)

Tab **Planned (Planificado)**. **Entry (Entrada)** adds a row dated a week from today; cells are
edited in place: **Date (dd-mm-yyyy) (Fecha (dd-mm-aaaa))**, **Description (Descripción)**, **Kind
(Tipo)**, **Site (Sede)**, **Currency (Moneda)** and **Amount (Monto)**. The delete icon removes
an entry after confirmation.

### Required information and prerequisites (Requisitos previos)

A description, an amount and a date. The site is optional: without one the entry goes to the
company lane.

### Business rules and rationale (Reglas y razón de negocio)

- Kinds: **Capital expenditure (Inversión (capex))**, **Dividend (Dividendos)** and **Other
  outflow (Otra salida)** take money out; **Capital contribution (Aporte de capital)** and **Other
  income (Otro ingreso)** bring it in. The amount is typed positive and the kind gives its sign.
- A row is saved as soon as it is complete, and every later edit saves it again; there is no save
  button.
- Only entries dated today or later count. A past entry shows its date in red, stops counting and
  raises a warning: move or delete it.

### Result and side effects (Resultado y efectos)

The entry appears in the Daily view as the **Planned (Planificado)** row of its site and in the
chart. It does not move any cash or create any accounting entry.

### Limitations (Limitaciones)

An entry is a single date: it does not repeat. Recurring payments belong in recurring bills
(`gastos programados`) of Expenses.

### Common questions and vocabulary (Preguntas y vocabulario)

- ¿Cómo agrego una compra futura al flujo? ¿Dónde pongo el reparto de dividendos? ¿Cómo registro
  un aporte de los socios?

## Related pages and workflows (Páginas y procesos relacionados)

- **Cash & Banks (Cajas & Bancos)** gives the opening cash; count each account regularly.
- **Loans (Préstamos)** feeds the loan installments.
- **Expenses (Gastos)** holds expenses and recurring bills; **Purchase orders (Órdenes de compra)**
  the payables; sales orders the receivables.
- **Payroll (Planilla)** and **Employees (Trabajadores)** feed payroll and contributions.
- **Tax Calendar (Calendario Tributario)** gives the SUNAT due dates; **Exchange rate (Tipo de
  cambio)** the dollar rate.
