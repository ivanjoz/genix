## 1. RCE — Registro de Compras Electrónico (SIRE)

### 1.1 How the RCE works

SUNAT builds a **monthly proposal** (*propuesta*) from the electronic documents that suppliers
issued to the company. It is available from the 2nd calendar day of the month. The taxpayer then
does one of three things:

| Option | Annex | What the ERP sends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accept** the proposal | — | Nothing. Filename code `01`. |
| **Complement** the proposal | **Anexo 8** | Add documents SUNAT did not propose. Move amounts SUNAT put in 15/16 into 17–20. Fix the exchange rate (27) or the classification (33). Exclude or re-include proposed documents. Filename `RRRRRRRRRRR-CP-AAAAMM-NN.txt`, or `-RCECOM-` / `-RCEINEX-`. |
| **Replace** the proposal | **Anexo 11** | The whole book, built by the ERP. Filename code `02`. **This is the file equivalent to our Ventas TXT export.** |

Later corrections to a book already generated use **Anexo 12**: the same layout plus the original
CAR in field 37, filename code `03` with a 2-digit correlative `NN`. Tabla 15 describes the
procedure.

**Which documents SUNAT proposes (Tabla 11, "Información de la propuesta" column):** only
`01` factura, `07`/`08` notas, `23`, `30`, `34`, `42`, `46`, `50`, `52`, `53` and `54`. These
**are not proposed** and must be added by the taxpayer: `02` recibo por honorarios, `03` boleta,
`12` ticket, `14` recibo de servicios públicos, `10` recibo de arrendamiento, and most other types.

> **Design consequence.** With **complement**, SUNAT supplies the facturas and the ERP only has
> to send the boletas, RH and recibos SP it holds. With **replace**, the ERP must hold *every*
> purchase document, or the book understates the month. The Diario needs the full data
> anyway, but the RCE upload can pick either path. This is an open decision (§6).

### 1.2 Filename (Tabla 13)

```
LE RRRRRRRRRRR AAAA MM 00 080400 CC O I M 2 [NN] .TXT
```

- Book identifier **`080400`** (8.4). `DD` = `00`. `G` = `2` (fixed, generated by SIRE).
- `CC`: `01` accept · `02` **replace** · `03` later adjustments · `04`/`05` adjustments to periods
  before SIRE (general / simplified format) · `00`/`06` non-domiciled.
- `O`: `0` RUC deregistered · `1` operating · `2` book closed. `I`: `1` with data · `0` without data.
  `M`: `1` soles · `2` dollars.
- Replacement mask: **`LERRRRRRRRRRRAAAAMM0008040002OIM2.TXT`**.
- 8.5 (`080500`) is the separate non-domiciled supplier book. Not relevant to a domestic SME.

### 1.3 General rules (Tabla 12)

- Separator `|`. Negative amounts are written `- #.##`.
- Free text must not contain `|`, `/` or `\`. "Alfanumérico" means A–Z, a–z, 0–9 and `( ) , . -`.
- Dates must be on or before the reported period.
- Identity documents (type → length / kind / fixed-or-variable / modulus 11):
  `0` 15 A V · `1` DNI 08 N F · `4` 12 A V · `6` RUC 11 N F **mod-11** · `7` 12 A V ·
  `A` 15 N F · `B`–`F` 15 A V · **`G` Salvoconducto 15 A V** (new in RS 138-2023).

### 1.4 Anexo 11 — the replacement file, field by field

note type, document status, inconsistencies. They are not part of the RCE and are **left out of
the file**. Anexo 8 note 8 describes the result as a 76-field structure: 1–37 plus 42–80.
**42–80** are free-use fields. If they are not used, leave out both the fields and their pipes.

Rules from the Anexo 11 notes:

- **Credit notes (`07`) go in negative** in fields 15–25, written `- #.##`. For the RCE this is
  explicit.
- **Documents with baja, reverted, or annulled by a type-02 credit note (wrong RUC) are NOT
  entered.** This is the **opposite of RVIE** nota 3, where they are entered at `0.00`.
- Only authorized documents (CDR accepted) are entered, and no duplicates.
- Σ(15, 17, 19) = the document's taxable base, and Σ(16, 18, 20) = its IGV. When a complement
  file is checked against the proposal, the tolerance is ±1.
- ISC on taxed lines goes into the base (15/17/19). ISC on untaxed lines goes into field 21.
- **Exchange rate (Anexo 8 note 4):** the **SBS weighted-average selling rate** published for the
  **issue date**. For credit/debit notes, the rate of the **modified document's** issue date.
  Legal basis: RIGV art. 5 num. 17.
- When SUNAT does not publish a rate for a currency, it is supplied through Anexo 10
  (`-RCETCA-` file). Not relevant for PEN/USD.

### 1.5 Document status (Tabla 14)

`1` Activo (CDR accepted) · `2` Baja · `3` Revertido (RH / liquidación de compra) · `4` Anulado
(credit note type 02, wrong RUC) · `5` Autorizado (printed with authorization) · `6` No
autorizado. It is only shown in field 40, which SUNAT fills. The ERP uses it to decide **which
rows to omit**.

### 1.6 Reference tables in the annex

- **Tabla 4** — customs office codes (DAM).
- **Tabla 10** — payment method: `001` depósito en cuenta … `008`/`009` efectivo … `999` otros.
  **The RCE file has no payment-method field**, so this table only feeds other formats.
- **Tabla 23** — classification: `1` mercadería / materia prima / suministros · `2` activo fijo ·
  `3` otros activos · `4` gastos de educación, recreación, representación… · `5` otros gastos.

## 2. CAR — Código de Anotación de Registro (Tabla 7 / Anexo 7)

27 characters: **issuer's RUC or ID (11) + document type (2) + series (4) + number (10)**.

- Left-pad with zeros. If a part is too long, keep its **rightmost** characters. An empty part
  becomes all zeros.
- For **purchases**, the issuer is the **supplier**, so the CAR starts with the supplier's RUC.
  For **sales**, it starts with the company's own RUC.
- Consolidated range of documents without crédito fiscal: use the **first document** of the range.
- Types `46`/`50`–`54` use the importer's or withholding agent's RUC, not the issuer's.
- SUNAT assigns it automatically from the proposal. It is deterministic, so the ERP can compute
  it. `accounting/types/sunat_car.go` already does this for sales.

**Implementation consequences:**

- **Every line carries a document reference** (fields 10 and 12 are mandatory). A line from an
  invoice uses its type, series and number. A line with no comprobante (depreciation, a cash
  transfer, apertura, cierre) uses `00` and an internal number.
- The key is period + CUO + correlative + **account + document number**. Inside one entry, the
  same account may repeat only with a different document number. **A daily consolidated entry
  needs a document number of its own** — for example `00` plus a day key. Its field 20 stays empty.
- Amounts go in the book's currency (the `M` flag in the filename; soles). **There is no
  exchange-rate field**, so a USD document must be converted before it is written. The rate source
  is the same open problem as RVIE field 28 and RCE field 27.
- The PLE checks that Debe equals Haber over the whole file. Balancing each CUO is still ours to
  unit-test.
- The account codes must be numeric and appear in 5.4. The chart of accounts is therefore a real
  list with codes and names, not only a mapping.

## 6. What this means for the implementation

### Libro de Compras

- **The RCE-annex stopper in PLAN §1.1 / §6 is closed.** The target is Anexo 11: `080400`,
  filename code `02`, fields 1–37.
- **Revisions to the column set approved in PLAN §2.2:**

  | PLAN column | Verdict |
  | --- | --- |
  | `DocType`, `DocSeries`, `DocNumber`, `DocIssueDate` | ✅ needed (fields 7, 8, 10, 5) |
  | `TaxableAmount`, `TaxAmount` | ✅ needed (15/16). For a company with only taxed sales, everything goes to DG. DGNG/DNG only matter with exonerated sales. |
  | `OtherAmount` | ✅ field 24 |
  | — | 🆕 **untaxed value** (exonerado + inafecto, field 21) is missing from the set |
  | `Currency`, `ExchangeRate` | ✅ fields 26/27. The rate must be the **SBS selling rate of the issue date**. |
  | `DetractionCode`, `DetractionDate` | ❓ **not in the RCE file** (field 38 is filled by SUNAT). Still useful for the date the crédito fiscal can be used, and for the Diario, but no longer required for the book. |
  | — | 🆕 **Due/payment date** (field 6): mandatory for type `14`. `Expense.DueDate` already exists. |
  | — | ❓ **Book period**: field 5 lets a document from an earlier month be entered later. Either the RCE period is derived from `DocIssueDate` (simple, the usual case), or a separate column is added. Decision needed. |

- **Rows to omit:** documents with baja, reverted, or annulled by type-02 credit note. Credit
  notes are negative.
- **Consolidation:** only for documents without crédito fiscal (supplier boletas, tickets). It's
  optional — detailed rows are always valid.
- **Replace vs complement** (§1.1): complement means the ERP only sends the documents SUNAT
  cannot see. That is less work and less risk of disagreeing with the proposal. Decision needed.

### Diario Simplificado

- Field 20 = CAR for non-consolidated entries, **supplier RUC** for purchase entries.
  `sunat_car.go` works for both.
- The screen view can be built now using the physical 5.2 layout (§3.2).
- The TXT is specified (§3.3). What it still needs is a **numeric chart of accounts with names**
  (5.4), and a PEN conversion rate for USD documents.

### Ventas (already built)

- Fix or confirm the `0` identity on detailed anonymous boletas (§4).
- Add `G` Salvoconducto to the identity catalog.
- Field 28's source must be the SBS weighted-average selling rate of the issue date.
  `SaleOrder.ExchangeRate` includes the store spread, and its fallback is the BCRP interbank rate,
  so it can't be used.
